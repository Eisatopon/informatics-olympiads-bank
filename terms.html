What you may do

Antikythera is free to use for personal study and for teaching: read the tasks, print task sets for your own students, export single tasks to LaTeX for your own work, and share links to the site.

What needs written permission

Without written permission from eisatopon.gr you may not: download the collection in bulk or with automated tools (scraping, crawling, mirroring); republish a substantial part of the bank on another website, app, book or database; build a derivative task bank from it; or use its content to train or evaluate artificial-intelligence systems.

What is protected

The transcriptions of the statements, the translations, the redrawn and cropped figures, the selection, classification and organisation of the tasks, the software and design of the site, and the database as a whole (the sui generis database right of EU Directive 96/9/EC and Greek law 2121/1993, articles 45A ff.). The tasks themselves remain the work and property of their authors and organising committees; Antikythera reproduces them for study, with the source of every task.

Organisers and corrections

Competition organisers may ask for corrections or removal of their tasks, and anyone may ask for permission to reuse content, through eisatopon.gr.

The published copy of the bank contains invisible marks that identify it.
